IN-APP RATING · APP STORE AND GOOGLE PLAY

How the rating card looks in our app

Both stores draw this card themselves; our app only asks for it at the right moment. Here it appears over a booked trip screen. When we ask is set out in the requirement page. Apple's is an alert in the middle of the screen; Google Play's is a sheet from the bottom.

Try both flows in the prototype

iPhone: App Store

Four steps. Up to 3 times a year per person, and Apple decides each time whether it shows.

Android: Google Play

Five steps. About once a month per person, and Google decides each time whether it shows.

Good to know

The card is the store's, not oursIts look and words come from Apple and Google, and shift a little between phone and OS versions. We cannot restyle it, cover it or add anything before it.
Sometimes nothing appearsIf the person has hit the store's limit, or rated already, the ask does nothing. The booking screen just carries on, with no error.
We never learn the resultNeither store tells the app whether the card showed or what rating was given. We count asks, and read new ratings in the store consoles.
"Rate 30 Sundays" in Account is differentA permanent row there opens the store page itself, not this card. It has no limit, and it is how someone rates whenever they want to.
