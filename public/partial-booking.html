REQUIREMENT · PARTLY BOOKED TRIPS · 23 NOV 2026

Partly booked: book the flights or hotels first

Today a trip is marked sold only when every part is final and the first instalment is paid. Now a customer can book part of it first, say the flights, and finish the hotels, day plans and visa later. The trip stays in My Plans, marked Partly booked. What is booked is locked with a clear reason, and its voucher can be downloaded. What is still open can be changed as usual.

Scope

In this release
  • Flights and hotels can be booked before the rest, in any order, all of them or only some.
  • The trip stays in My Plans with a Partly booked tag, until every part is booked.
  • On the trip: each booked flight and hotel is locked, with a lock, a Booked tag and an i that says why.
  • The trip's dates, cities, travellers (and departure city, once flights to and from India are booked) are fixed. Customise is not shown.
  • Vouchers for everything booked: on the card, in the flight details, on the hotel page.
  • A Payment section: what was paid and when, what is still to come, and the consent accepted.
  • Confirm and pay, with consent, when the consultant sends a part to be booked.
Not in this release
  • Booking day plans, activities or the visa on their own. They are booked with the last part.
  • The customer changing or cancelling a booked item in the app. They talk to the consultant.
  • More than one partly booked plan per customer.
  • The seller side (Mark Sold, consent link, price re-check). See Jaikishan's prototype.

Customer-facing words always say "booked", never "sold".

The rules

  1. One partly booked plan. A customer has at most one plan that is partly booked.
  2. It stays in My Plans. A partly booked trip does not move to My Trips. It moves there once every part is booked, exactly as a fully sold trip does today.
  3. The basics are fixed. Once anything is booked: dates, cities and nights, and travellers. Once a flight to or from India is booked: the departure city too. Customise is not shown on the trip, and so the route, dates and travellers cannot be changed.
  4. Booked items are locked. A booked flight has no Change or Remove and no fare. A booked hotel has no Change hotel and its page opens read-only. The upgrade banner hides once every hotel is booked.
  5. Open items work as today. Hotels and flights not yet booked keep Change, Remove and Change hotel. Day plans can be changed. Saving makes a new version as usual; the bookings carry over to it.
  6. While a consent link is out, nothing changes. The parts sent show To confirm, everything else On hold, until the customer confirms and pays. To change something first, the consultant withdraws the link.
  7. Every booked item has its voucher. Shown once the voucher is issued.

1. My Plans

  • The card keeps today's format. A green ✓ Partly booked tag sits on its own line under the dates, so the route and dates stay clear. No other line is added.
  • Partly booked trips sit at the top of My Plans, with the plans waiting on payment.
  • While a consent link is out, the card shows today's "Confirmation and payment pending" note. Its button opens the trip straight on the consent screen.
  • Version list and Compare work as today.

2. Top of the trip

  • No Customise button and no summary of what is booked: each section says it where it matters.
  • The dates, travellers and route read as today, with nothing to tap.
  • Only while a consent link is out: one amber strip, "Confirmation and payment pending", with Confirm and pay.

3. Flights

Every flight booked

  • Said once, at the top of the section, on the "Flying from" line: 🔒 All flights booked ⓘ. Change city is gone.
  • The cards show no fare and no Change or Remove. Each has Download voucher above "Tap to view flight details".

Some flights booked

  • A booked flight shows 🔒 Booked ⓘ where its fare was, no Change or Remove, and Download voucher.
  • A flight not booked keeps its fare, Change and Remove, and can be changed as today.
  • If the flights to and from India are booked, the departure city is fixed (no Change city). If only flights inside the country are booked, it is not.

The i, and the details

  • Tapping the tag shows a short note (copy below). Any other tap or a scroll puts it away.
  • The flight details sheet of a booked flight shows the Booked tag in place of the fare, and a Download voucher button.

4. Hotels

Every hotel booked

  • Said once, beside the "Hotels" heading: 🔒 All hotels booked ⓘ. The photos carry no tag.
  • Each card has Download voucher in place of Change hotel. The upgrade banner is not shown.

Some hotels booked

  • A booked hotel has 🔒 Booked ⓘ on its photo, and Download voucher in place of Change hotel.
  • A hotel not booked keeps Change hotel, as today.

The hotel page

  • A booked hotel's page opens read-only: no room choice, no Change hotel. Its bottom bar says "We have booked this hotel for you" with the Booked tag, and a Download voucher button.

5. Payment and consent

  • A Payment section under the Cost breakdown, on a partly booked trip only.
  • One row per booking: "Paid on 14 Nov 2026 · For 2 flights · ₹1,02,000". A trip booked in two parts has two rows.
  • "Finalise later · For 1 hotel, day plans, visa · tentative" with the rest of the total. The rows add up to the trip's final price.
  • "Consent and payment terms accepted on 21 Nov 2026 · View" opens what was agreed, read-only.
  • "Instalments are set once every part is booked."

6. Confirm and pay

Follows the consent link in Jaikishan's prototype, inside the app.

  1. The consultant sends a part to be booked. The card in My Plans and the top of the trip show Confirmation and payment pending.
  2. Confirm and pay opens a full screen: "Your Vietnam 3 flights are ready to book", the dates, travellers and departure city.
  3. Your itinerary, in the same three groups the consultant sees in the itinerary builder: Already finalised (booked earlier, with a lock; shown only if something is), Finalise now (each flight or hotel being sent, opened) and Finalise later (what is still open). Then "We cannot honour verbal or informal commitments". Viewed after paying, what was agreed shows under Already finalised.
  4. Traveller details, as filled in by our team, to check against each passport. Each traveller is a card that opens to show first name, last name, date of birth and gender; the date of birth is not repeated on the closed card. A traveller with no passport on file gets one pill, "Passport pending", and inside the card one short note: "Check these against your passport. Fixing them later can cost extra." The "All details captured" pill by the heading shows only when every traveller has a passport, so the same thing is never flagged twice. The blue "What counts as a valid passport?" dropdown is the last thing in the card.
  5. Invoicing details: the PAN the invoice and TCS go in, with a Verified pill by the heading. Its blue "Why do we need this?" dropdown is also the last thing in the card. Every FAQ dropdown on this screen sits at the bottom of its card, never above the content.
  6. Payment schedule, its own card: the same three names: "Already finalised" with what was paid before (only if anything was), "Finalise now, pay now" with the amount and what it is for, then "Finalise later" with the rest, marked tentative, then "Instalments are set once every part is booked." The last thing in the card is the blue link "Read full Terms and Conditions", which opens the full terms.
  7. Cancellation policy, a separate card: flights cannot be refunded once ticketed; hotels follow each hotel's own policy. Only the lines for what is being booked show.
  8. Your consent: one tick, "I confirm and agree to all of the below", covering the items being final, the trip's basics being fixed, the traveller details, and the terms. Continue to pay stays grey until it is ticked.
  9. Your payment: the amount due today and what it is for. Pay online is the one main button, full width and pink, with "UPI, card or net banking" under it and a blue "Copy payment link for someone else". A line says the rest is priced and paid when it is finalised. Pay by bank transfer instead sits below, folded away; opened, it shows the account details with copy buttons and a plain outline button, "I have made the transfer". Two pink buttons of the same weight are never shown together.
  10. Bank transfer: "We are checking your transfer. We book your 3 flights once it reaches us, usually within a working day." Nothing is booked, and the trip keeps waiting, until the money is matched.
  11. Paid online: "Your 3 flights are booked. We have sent the details on WhatsApp and email." Back on the trip, they show Booked, with vouchers once issued.
