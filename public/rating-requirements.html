Ask for a store rating at the right moment

Show the App Store or Google Play rating card inside our app, at three happy moments, and never when something has just gone wrong. The card belongs to the store: people tap the stars and submit without leaving the app. The goal is to take both store ratings to 4.5.

Scope

In this release
  • Show the store's rating card at three moments, iPhone and Android.
  • Skip the ask when a warning sign is present.
  • Ask each person once from our side, and remember it on the phone.
  • A permanent "Rate 30 Sundays" row in Account that opens the store page.
  • Stop today's in-app feedback prompt from firing right after login and repeating.
  • Events for every ask and every tap on the Account row.
Not in this release
  • Our own star screen in front of the store card. Both stores forbid it.
  • Any reward, discount or points for rating.
  • Resetting the App Store rating. It would wipe the 68 ratings we have.
  • Replies to store reviews from inside our tools (done in the store consoles).

How often

  • Once per person from our side. After we ask, we never ask that person again, on any moment. Every repeat ask in our data rated lower.
  • The store has its own limit on top. Apple shows the card at most 3 times a year per person. Google shows it about once a month, on a limit it does not publish. When the store says no, nothing appears and the app just carries on.
  • Remember it for the account, not only the phone. Save "asked on" with the date, moment and app version on the phone, and on the server against the customer, so a reinstall or a second phone does not ask again.
  • Never from a button. The card may not appear, which would look broken. Buttons open the store page instead (see the Account row).

Google Play: In-App Review library

What it does
Opens Google Play's rating sheet over our app. The person picks 1 to 5 stars, can add a public review, and submits without leaving.
Dependency
com.google.android.play:review:2.0.2 (latest), plus review-ktx for Kotlin. This is the standalone library; the old all-in-one Play Core library is retired.
Requirements
Android 5.0 (API 21) or newer, Play Store installed, app installed from Play. No permission needed.
Steps
1. Create a ReviewManager. 2. Call requestReviewFlow() to get a ReviewInfo, only just before showing, as it expires. 3. Call launchReviewFlow(activity, reviewInfo). 4. When it completes, carry on; it completes whether or not anything was shown.
Limits
Quota is per person and unpublished, about once a month. The sheet cannot be resized, covered, restyled or closed by us. Nothing on screen should invite the tap. New ratings show in the Play Console after about 24 hours.

Overview · Integrate (Kotlin, Java) · Test · Release notes

Native Android, for reference (Kotlin)

// build.gradle
implementation("com.google.android.play:review:2.0.2")
implementation("com.google.android.play:review-ktx:2.0.2")

// When a moment is reached and every check passes
val manager = ReviewManagerFactory.create(context)
manager.requestReviewFlow().addOnCompleteListener { request ->
    if (request.isSuccessful) {
        manager.launchReviewFlow(activity, request.result)
            .addOnCompleteListener { /* carry on, no result is given */ }
    }
    // On failure: do nothing, never show an error
}

Apple: StoreKit review request

What it does
Opens Apple's "Enjoying 30 Sundays?" alert. The person taps a star and submits, and can then write a review. It is the only rating prompt Apple allows (guideline 5.6.1).
Dependency
None. StoreKit ships with iOS.
Calls
AppStore.requestReview(in: windowScene) on iOS 16+, or @Environment(\.requestReview) (RequestReviewAction) in SwiftUI. The older SKStoreReviewController.requestReview(in:) is deprecated from iOS 18; it still works but should only be the fallback for iOS 14 to 15.
Limits
At most 3 times per person in 365 days. Someone who already rated sees it again only on a new app version and a year after their last review. People can switch these prompts off for every app in Settings. Apple decides each time whether it shows.
Write a review link
https://apps.apple.com/app/id6749570798?action=write-review for the Account row. Allowed, and has no limit.
Docs
Requesting App Store reviews · RequestReviewAction · SKStoreReviewController · Ratings and reviews (HIG)

Ask at a moment

import 'package:in_app_review/in_app_review.dart';

final _review = InAppReview.instance;

Future<void> askForStoreRating(String moment) async {
  if (!await RatingGuard.canAsk(moment)) return;   // checks below
  if (!await _review.isAvailable()) return;
  await Future.delayed(const Duration(seconds: 2));  // let the screen settle
  await _review.requestReview();                     // the store decides
  await RatingGuard.markAsked(moment);               // never ask again
  track('rating_prompt_requested', {'moment': moment});
}

Decision logic

Runs when Home, the trip screen or an itinerary has settled. All must pass, in this order, before the store is asked.

class RatingGuard {
  static Future<bool> canAsk(String moment) async {
    final s = await RatingState.load();   // phone + server copy

    if (s.everAsked) return false;                      // once per person
    if (s.isFirstAppOpen || s.inLoginOrOnboarding) return false;
    if (s.feedbackPromptShownThisSession) return false;
    if (s.lastSupportContact.within(days: 3)) return false;
    if (s.lastPaymentScreen.within(hours: 24)) return false;
    if (s.lastErrorShown.within(hours: 24)) return false;
    if (s.bookingCancelled || s.refundOpen || s.paymentFailed) return false;

    switch (moment) {
      case 'just_booked':      // 1 to 7 days after booking
        return s.isBooked && s.daysSinceBooking.between(1, 7);
      case 'trip_coming_up':   // trip 8 to 60 days away
        return s.isBooked && s.daysToTrip.between(8, 60);
      case 'engaged_lead':     // itinerary seen on a later day, 10s on it
        return !s.isBooked && s.appDaysCount >= 2
            && s.secondsOnItinerary >= 10 && Remote.leadMomentOn;
    }
    return false;
  }
}

Testing

Android
  • Upload to the internal test track and install from the Play Store. The quota does not apply there.
  • Use a Google account that has not reviewed 30 Sundays.
  • Internal app sharing shows the sheet, but Submit stays off.
  • Unit tests: FakeReviewManager stands in, shows nothing.
  • A debug build installed from a computer may show nothing. That is expected.
iPhone
  • A development build on a phone or simulator always shows the alert, and Submit does nothing.
  • TestFlight never shows it. Do not log that as a bug.
  • Test the Account row on a real iPhone; the simulator has no App Store.
  • On a live build, Apple decides; it may not show even when we ask.

QA tests our logic (does the app ask at the right moment, and not when a warning sign is present) by watching the rating_prompt_requested event, not by waiting for the card.

Store rules we must follow

Breaking these can get the app pulled or the developer account closed.

Watch every week

  • New ratings and their average, per store, in Play Console and App Store Connect (India).
  • Asks per moment, and skips per reason, from our events.
  • Two weeks after launch: if the store average of new ratings is below 4.5, drop the weakest moment (likely the lead one).
  • Every 1 to 3 star review replied to within 2 days.

Acceptance criteria

  • A booked customer opening the app 1 to 7 days after booking is asked once, 2 seconds after Home or the trip screen settles.
  • A booked customer whose trip is 8 to 60 days away is asked once, if not asked before.
  • A lead is asked on an itinerary only on a later day than their first, after 10 seconds, and only while the remote switch is on.
  • No ask within 3 days of a support or expert contact, 24 hours of a payment screen or an error, during the trip or the 7 days before it, or after the trip starts.
  • No ask on first open, during login or onboarding, or in a session where our feedback prompt showed.
  • After one ask, the same person is never asked again, after reinstall or on a second phone too.
  • Nothing breaks or shows an error when the store shows no card.
  • The person returns to the same screen, same scroll, after the card closes.
  • The Account row opens Write a Review on iPhone and our Play page on Android.
  • Our in-app feedback prompt no longer shows at login and shows at most once per person.
  • All three events fire with their properties.
