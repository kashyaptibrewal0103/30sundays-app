Plan together: invite someone to plan a trip

The person who made a trip invites their partner, family or friends. The invite goes on WhatsApp. The guest logs in with the invited number, is told who invited them, and finds the trip in My Plans next to their own. Both can change it, and every version says who saved it and when.

Scope

In this release
  • Invite from the trip screen, from contacts or by typing a name and number.
  • 30 Sundays sends the invite on WhatsApp. The owner can also send it from their own WhatsApp.
  • The guest logs in as usual with the invited number. A short screen says who invited them, then they land in My Plans.
  • Invited trips are marked in My Plans with who invited them.
  • Everyone on the trip can change it. Every version says who saved it and when.
  • The owner is told when a guest joins. The owner can remove people; a guest can leave.
Not in this release
  • Reminders. To nudge someone, the owner sends the invite again from their own WhatsApp.
  • Editing a number. A wrong number is removed and invited again.
  • An activity log. Who did what is shown on the versions instead.
  • A welcome screen before login. Login stays exactly as today.
  • Guests inviting other people, asking to join from another number, live co-editing, chat, split payments.

Inviting someone gives them access to plan. It does not change the number of travellers on the trip, or its price.

Who can do what

A trip has one owner, the person who made it, and up to 7 co-travellers (8 people in all).

1. The owner invites

Plan together appears on a saved trip only. It is not shown on the two unsaved options after the dates, on a closed plan, or on someone else's trip.

  1. Two ways in. A new line under the route at the top of the trip: "Plan this trip with your partner · Invite". And the Plan together section further down, where it is today.
  2. Invite someone. A sheet that leads with Pick from contacts, then Name and Mobile number (+91 by default, a country picker for numbers abroad). A contact with two numbers asks which one.
  3. Checks. Own number: "That's your number. Add someone else's." Already added: "Priya is already on this trip." Full: "A trip can have up to 8 people. Remove someone to add more." Short number: "Enter a 10-digit mobile number."
  4. Send invite. 30 Sundays sends the WhatsApp. The sheet confirms it and offers "Send from your WhatsApp", which opens the owner's WhatsApp on the guest's chat with the message filled in. "Copy invite link" sits under it.
  5. Back on the trip. The line under the route shows the faces and "Priya invited · not joined yet".
  6. People on this trip. Tapping it lists the owner, then each person with their number, status (Invited or Joined) and when. An invited person has a WhatsApp button to send it again from the owner's WhatsApp. The menu has one action: Remove from trip.

2. The guest joins

The number gives the guest the trip, not the link. A lost link or a fresh install never loses the invite. Login itself is unchanged.

  1. The WhatsApp. From 30 Sundays, from the owner, or both. Its button opens the app if installed, else the App Store or Play Store; on a laptop, a page with a QR code.
  2. Login as usual. The same mobile number and OTP screen as today. No invite screen before it.
  3. One screen after login. "Kashyap invited you to plan a Bali trip", with the trip and its latest version. One button: Go to My Plans. The guest is Joined from this moment and the owner is told.
  4. My Plans. The invited trip sits with their own trips. Its card has a line across the top, "Kashyap invited you to plan this trip", and is outlined and marked NEW until they open it.
  5. The trip. The same screen the owner sees, with a one-time tip: "This is the plan Kashyap made. Change anything and save. Kashyap will see it with your name." The line under the route reads "Planning with Kashyap".
  6. People on this trip. The guest sees everyone, cannot invite or remove, and can Leave this trip.

After this, the rest of the app works as it does today.

4. Versions say who saved them

  1. Unsaved changes belong to the person making them. Nobody else sees them.
  2. Saving creates a new version, as today. Versions are never changed after they are saved.
  3. Every version carries who saved it and when: "V3 · Priya · Today, 4:12 pm". Your own say "you". The consultant's say "30 Sundays".
  4. The My Plans card keeps today's format. Who saved a version shows only once the earlier versions are opened: each row reads "Saved by Priya · latest", "Saved by you", with the time and price.
  5. On the trip it shows on the line under the route ("V3 by Priya · Today, 4:12 pm"), in What changed ("Priya made V3 from V2"), and in Compare.
  6. When someone saves, everyone else on the trip gets a push: "Priya saved V3 of your Bali trip".
  7. Two people editing at once. Both start from V2. Priya saves first, so V3 is Priya's. Kashyap sees "Priya just saved V3. Your changes will save as V4." When Kashyap saves, that is V4. Nothing is merged or lost; Compare shows both.

My Plans: own trips and invited trips

  • One list, as today. Trips they made and trips they were invited to sit together.
  • An invited trip has a line across the top of its card: the inviter's face and "Kashyap invited you to plan this trip".
  • A new invite is outlined in pink and marked NEW until opened, and sits at the top of the list until then.
  • The count under the title says how many are shared: "3 plans · 2 shared with you".
  • Otherwise the card is today's card, unchanged: photo, destination and nights, version, route, dates and travellers, Generated, price, and the earlier versions folded under it.
  • Opening the earlier versions shows who saved each one, the latest included: "V3 · Saved by you · latest", "V2 · Saved by Ananya · 15 Nov". Who saved what is not on the closed card.
  • The owner's card is today's card with nothing added. When the owner opens the versions, the ones Priya saved say "Saved by Priya".

Privacy, sales and back office

  • Shared with everyone on the trip: the trip, every version and who saved it, the prices, first names and photos of the people on it.
  • Kept private: each person's other trips, phone numbers (only the owner sees the numbers they typed), receipts and payment methods of others, passport and traveller details of others.
  • CRM: a guest logging in for the first time must not create a new lead. They are added to the owner's deal as a co-traveller. The consultant sees who is on the trip and who saved each version. The owner stays the main contact.
  • To store: for each invite, the trip, who invited, the name and number typed, the status, how it was sent, and when it was sent, joined or removed. For each version, who saved it as well as when. Times in Indian time.
