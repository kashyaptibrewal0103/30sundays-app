PICK YOUR TRIP SCREEN

Three looks, side by side

The screen that shows the two trips and the way to choose your own cities, designed three ways. Each one is built in the prototype and leads to the same trip, the same switch and the same save. Look B was chosen and is the screen in the flow now. The other two are kept here to compare.

  • Everything in view at onceNo carousel. Options hidden behind a swipe get missed, so all three choices are on the first screen.
  • Own cities is a real choiceIt sits beside the two trips at the same size, not as a line of small print under them.
  • Show the difference, do not list itThe nights of each trip are a bar, cut where the hotel changes. Short stays against long ones read in a glance.
  • One clear next stepOne trip starts picked, and the button always says what it will open.
